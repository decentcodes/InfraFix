# InfraFix 🏙️

**Report local infrastructure issues. Track progress. Help build safer communities.**

InfraFix is a location-based civic infrastructure reporting and priority management platform designed to connect citizens with the authorities responsible for maintaining public infrastructure.

From potholes and damaged streetlights to water leakages and blocked drainage, InfraFix provides a structured way to report local problems, discover nearby issues, and help authorities identify which problems require attention first.

## 🌍 Why InfraFix?

Civic infrastructure problems can affect public safety, accessibility, and everyday life. However, managing these issues can be challenging when reports are scattered, duplicate complaints accumulate, or urgent hazards compete with routine maintenance requests.

InfraFix aims to make this process more organised through location-based reporting, issue tracking, administrative coordination, and context-aware prioritisation.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Location-Based Reporting:** Report infrastructure problems associated with their geographical locations.
- **Issue Discovery:** Explore nearby reported problems and understand the issues affecting your surroundings.
- **Duplicate Report Management:** Identify related reports to reduce unnecessary duplication and consolidate information about the same issue.
- **Intelligent Prioritisation:** Evaluate issues using multiple factors, including public safety, severity, location criticality, number of reports, duration, and weather conditions.
- **Authority Coordination:** Connect reported issues with the appropriate authorities based on administrative jurisdiction and issue category.
- **Issue Tracking:** Follow the progress of reported problems through their resolution lifecycle.
- **Administrative Management:** Support the management of authorities, responsibilities, and issue assignments.

## 🔄 How InfraFix Works

### 1. Report an Issue
A citizen identifies an infrastructure problem, selects its category, provides a description, and shares its location. Supporting photographs can help communicate the problem.

### 2. Validate and Consolidate Reports
The system checks submitted information and identifies potential duplicate reports. Related reports can contribute to a consolidated understanding of an existing issue.

### 3. Identify the Responsible Authority
The issue is associated with the appropriate authority according to its category and geographical jurisdiction.

### 4. Determine Priority
The system evaluates the issue using multiple factors rather than relying solely on complaint counts.

Public safety receives the greatest consideration. For example, an exposed electrical wire or an open manhole during heavy rainfall may require urgent attention even when fewer citizens have reported it.

### 5. Review and Resolve
The responsible authority reviews the issue, manages its status, and records progress towards resolution.

### 6. Track Progress
Citizens can follow the issue's status, helping make the reporting and resolution process more visible.

## ⚖️ A Context-Aware Approach to Prioritisation

Not all infrastructure problems carry the same level of risk.

InfraFix considers the following factors when evaluating issue priority:

| Factor | What it considers |
|---|---|
| **Safety** | Potential risk of injury or harm to the public |
| **Severity** | Seriousness and extent of the problem |
| **Location Criticality** | Importance of the affected location, such as areas near schools and hospitals |
| **Number of Reports** | Reporting activity associated with the issue |
| **Duration** | How long the issue has remained unresolved |
| **Weather Conditions** | Environmental conditions that may increase the risk |

By considering these factors together, InfraFix aims to support more informed and safety-conscious decisions about infrastructure maintenance.

## 👥 Who Is InfraFix For?

- **Citizens:** Report problems, discover nearby issues, and track progress.
- **Local Authorities:** Review assigned issues, manage priorities, and coordinate resolutions.
- **Administrators:** Manage authority access, responsibilities, and administrative operations.

## 🎯 Project Vision

InfraFix aims to contribute to a more transparent, organised, and responsive approach to civic infrastructure management, where problems are easier to report, urgent hazards receive appropriate attention, and citizens can better understand the progress of their complaints.

## 🚧 Project Status

InfraFix is an ongoing project being developed iteratively. Features and workflows are subject to refinement as implementation and testing progress. The current citizen OTP implementation is development-only. The geographic region covered in this version is "Delhi" and it's 11 districts.
